# Live in Labs — Village Field Study
### Naala, Uttarakhand

## About
A community-based field study conducted as part of 
Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham's Live in Labs programme.

## What we did
- Applied PRA (Participatory Rural Appraisal) and 
  HCD (Human-Centred Design) tools to identify 
  water and health challenges in the village
- Co-implemented a Jivamritam water filtration system 
  with local stakeholders
- Conducted health and wellbeing awareness sessions

## Takeaway
Technology-driven social impact — understanding 
a real problem before building a solution.
