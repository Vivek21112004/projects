# Climber Bot — Tele-Operated Mobile Manipulator
### IDC Robocon 2024, Kerala, India

## What it does
A differential-drive robot with a 3-DOF arm and gripper, 
designed for coconut harvesting. Controlled wirelessly 
via a 6-channel RC interface.

## Hardware
- Arduino Mega
- Motor drivers (L298N)
- Servo motors (3-DOF arm)
- 6-channel RC receiver
- Custom chassis (fabricated on-site)

## Software
- Language: Arduino (C++)
- Features: dead-zones, safety stops, 
  fast/precision arm switching modes

## Competition
Built and tested within 12 days at IDC Robocon 2024.
Collaborated with international students on design, 
fabrication, and programming.
